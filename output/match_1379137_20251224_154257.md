# Alexander Isak: Fears Liverpool striker sustained broken leg against Tottenham

Liverpool fear record-signing Alexander Isak sustained a suspected broken leg in
Saturday’s 2-1 win over Tottenham Hotspur.

The 26-year-old, a half-time replacement for Conor Bradley, was injured in the
act of opening the scoring in the 56th minute with a smart finish from Florian
Wirtz’s pass. After striking the ball, he was brought down awkwardly by a
sliding challenge from Spurs defender Micky van de Ven and was unable to
celebrate with his teammates.

Advertisement

Isak received treatment and was helped off the pitch by two members of
Liverpool’s medical staff, with Jeremie Frimpong replacing him.

Liverpool are concerned at the extent of the damage caused to the forward’s
lower leg with the extent of the injury yet to be determined, but typically
results in at least a few months out of action.

_The Athletic_ previously reported that the club feared that the 26-year-old had
sustained a significant leg injury.

## Will Isak injury force Liverpool to be active in January?

_Analysis by Liverpool reporter Andy Jones_

Alexander Isak’s first few months at Liverpool have been forgettable but just
when it looked like he had turned a corner, he has suffered another major
setback.

Advertisement

As he netted his second Premier League goal of the season to give Liverpool the
lead against Tottenham he was unable to celebrate. The agonising look on his
face told the story as he clutched his leg and he hobbled off soon after. Now,
it seems he has suffered a broken leg, which would usually mean months out of
action.

After becoming a record-breaking signing in the summer when he arrived at
Anfield for £125million from Newcastle United, fitness and form have both been
issues.

But being ruled out for a lengthy spell would be a huge blow for him as an
individual, but also have knock-on effects for head coach Arne Slot and his
depth in attack.

Slot is fortunate that he can turn to another elite No 9 in Hugo Ekitike. The
23-year-old is in excellent form, scoring five goals in his last three league
games.

However, with Salah away at the Africa Cup of Nations and his future up in the
air and Cody Gakpo on his way back from a muscle injury, his attacking options
in the short team include only Federico Chiesa and 17-year-old Rio Ngumoha.
Neither have started a league game this season.

With Salah away, this had been earmarked as an opportunity for Isak and Ekitike
to get a run in the team, potentially together. Those plans now lie in tatters
and Slot will need to manage Ekitike carefully.

There has to be a chance that Liverpool may now be forced to move in the January
transfer window. A versatile forward who can play as a No 9 and in wide areas
would make more sense that a like-for-like replacement as attacking depth in
wide areas is also low.

Finding a player of the required level and for a sensible fee will be the
challenge. Antoine Semenyo, who has a £65million release clause, is admired by
Liverpool and can operate on both flanks but has only played a handful of games
as a No.9 for Bournemouth.

[Liverpool](https://www.espn.com/soccer/team?id=364) forward [Alexander
Isak](http://espn.com/soccer/player/_/id/235662/alexander-isak) could be facing
a lengthy spell on the sideline after suffering an injury while scoring in his
team's [Premier League](https://www.espn.com/soccer/league/_/name/ENG.1) win
over [Tottenham Hotspur](https://www.espn.com/soccer/team?id=367) on Saturday.

Isak was introduced as a halftime substitute in north London and opened the
scoring against 10-man Spurs with a cool finish in the 56th minute.

However, the Sweden international was immediately forced off after a coming
together with Tottenham defender [Micky van de
Ven](http://espn.com/soccer/player/_/id/296512/micky-van-de-ven) inside the
penalty area in which he appeared to injure his right knee.

Alexander Isak limped off after scoring against Tottenham Hotspur. Photo by
Liverpool FC/Liverpool FC via Getty Images

He did not need a stretcher but looked in a lot of pain as he hobbled off the
pitch, with Liverpool now facing an anxious wait to discover the full extent of

Read the latest information on injuries, suspensions and availability in the
Liverpool men's squad.

​​​​​​ _Last updated on December 13_

  * ## **Injuries**

**Wataru Endo**

The midfielder has been out of action with an ankle injury since appearing in
the 3-3 draw at Leeds United on December 6.

Arne Slot said his absence will be "weeks" when providing an update on December
12.

**Jeremie Frimpong**

Frimpong was forced off during the early stages of the Champions League win over
Eintracht Frankfurt in October.

Slot reported on December 13 that "normally Jeremie Frimpong will train with us
[next week]".

**Cody Gakpo**

Gakpo is currently sidelined with a muscle problem. As with Endo, speaking on
December 12 Slot said the issue will rule him out for "weeks".

**Joe Gomez**

The No.2 was forced off during the first half of the victory over Brighton &
Hove Albion with a muscle concern.

Slot on December 13: "If I have a player who goes off with a muscle injury like
Joe, that's normally not a positive thing. I would not expect him to be in the
squad next week [at Tottenham Hotspur]. But sometimes you get a positive
surprise."

**Giovanni Leoni**

The defender is amid a lengthy spell on the sidelines after suffering an
anterior cruciate ligament injury at the end of his debut against Southampton in
the Carabao Cup.

Slot on September 26: "He is not in a good place, of course, because he tore his
ACL and it means he will be out for a year. Being so young and coming to a new
country and playing so well in your first game... it's very hard to take the
positive side.

"There's never a positive side – but you always try to look at a positive side
and that is that he is still so young, so he has so many years still to go after
he recovers from a terrible injury like that."

**Dominik Szoboszlai**

Szoboszlai was withdrawn in the closing stages of the win against Brighton due
to an ankle injury.

Slot on December 13: "It didn't look great when I just saw him. But he has
unbelievable mentality so let's hope for the best."

  * ## **Availability**

**Mohamed Salah**

After featuring off the bench versus Brighton on December 13, the forward
departs to represent Egypt at the Africa Cup of Nations.

His country's group-stage games are against Zimbabwe (December 22), South Africa
(December 26) and Angola (December 29).

  * ## **Suspensions**

**Conor Bradley** served a one-game Premier League suspension for the clash with
Brighton on December 13, after picking up his fifth caution of the season in the
draw at Leeds.

He therefore becomes available once more for the trip to Tottenham on December
20.

Bradley is also one yellow card away from incurring a one-match suspension in
the Champions League.

Meanwhile, **Dominik Szoboszlai** is one caution away from serving a one-match
suspension in the Premier League.

[Liverpool](https://www.standard.co.uk/topic/liverpool) will look to continue
their upward trajectory when they play Wolves after Christmas.

The Reds appear to have steadied the ship after a rocky spell, [with victory
over Tottenham on Saturday marking the first time they have won back-to-back
Premier League matches since
September](https://www.standard.co.uk/sport/football/tottenham-vs-liverpool-fc-
premier-league-b1263445.html "Tottenham 1-2 Liverpool: Hugo Ekitike on target
again as Reds beat nine-man Spurs").

Liverpool will be heavy favourites to pick up another three points when they
play rock-bottom Wolves on December 27, as they bid to close the distance to
Arsenal, Manchester City and Aston Villa at the top of the table.

But their 2-1 win over nine-man Tottenham [came at a cost with as many as three
new injury concerns emerging from their trip to the north
London](https://www.standard.co.uk/sport/football/liverpool-fc-injury-latest-
isak-wirtz-bradley-slot-b1263469.html "Liverpool dealt new triple injury worry
as Arne Slot issues Alexander Isak update").

[Alexander Isak](https://www.standard.co.uk/topic/alexander-isak) is the biggest
concern, [having undergone surgery on a broken
leg](https://www.standard.co.uk/sport/football/isak-injury-update-liverpool-
fc-b1263602.html "Liverpool confirm Alexander Isak injury blow as record signing
set for months on sidelines") suffered against Tottenham. He will now miss much
of the remainder of the season.

With that in mind, here is the latest [Liverpool injury
news](https://www.standard.co.uk/topic/liverpool-injury-news) and return dates…

## **Alexander Isak**

Isak lasted just 11 minutes after coming on as a substitute against Tottenham on
Saturday, picking up a serious injury after being caught by Micky van de Ven in
the act of giving Liverpool a 1-0 lead.

Isak was not able to celebrate his third goal of the season and, after receiving
lengthy treatment, was carried off the pitch with the help of Liverpool's
medical staff.

Slot said afterwards that it was "not a good thing" that Isak could not continue
and it was soon after reported that the £125million signing had suffered a bone
fracture.

Liverpool confirmed on Monday that Isak fractured his ankle during Saturday's
victory over Tottenham and has undergone surgery. He is expected to be sidelined
for many months, but could feature again this season.

A club statement read: "After diagnosis, an operation was completed today on an
ankle injury that included a fibula fracture.

"Isak's rehabilitation will now continue at the AXA Training Centre, with no
timeframe yet placed on his return."

_Potential return date: Unknown_

## **Conor Bradley**

Conor Bradley made his return to the Liverpool side at Spurs after missing the
win over Brighton.

But the right-back had to be replaced at half-time as he appeared to tweak his
hamstring while chasing Djed Spence when the Spurs defender pushed forward.

Slot said: “With Alex and with Conor, we have to wait. You could see our bench
today but this is not the first time. We are not the only team that suffers
injuries in this period in England because we play so many games.

“It is quite normal that teams pick up injuries at this time. We’re still
picking up points and that is the main thing.”

Slot will be hopeful Bradley’s injury is not too serious.

_Potential return date: Unknown_

## **Florian Wirtz**

The third Liverpool injury worry to emerge from the win over Tottenham was
[Florian Wirtz](https://www.standard.co.uk/topic/florian-wirtz).

Wirtz claimed an assist for Isak's goal but was spotted rubbing his hamstring
before making way for youngster Trey Nyoni as the visitors ran down the clock.

There was not much of an update on the German playmaker after game, and his
substitution appeared to be precautionary as the Reds are already light in the
attacking department.

_Potential return date: Saturday, December 27 vs Wolves (H)_

## Jeremie Frimpong

Jeremie Frimpong made his first appearance in two months as he returned from
injury as a 60th-minute substitute at Spurs.

He was then substituted himself in the 89th minute after going down, but Slot
allayed any fresh injury fears.

“No there is no problem at all,” he said. “I did not want to go down for 30
seconds.”

_Potential return date: Saturday, December 27 vs Wolves (H)_

## **Cody Gakpo**

Cody Gakpo has now missed Liverpool's last three games in all competitions with
a muscle injury sustained in the 3-3 draw against Leeds.

But, speaking ahead of the Spurs, game Slot confirmed Gakpo is making good
progress and could be back earlier than expected.

“He [Gakpo] had a scan that looked promising so that means that we are not that
worried anymore, as we were maybe one or two weeks ago.

“So that means he might come back a bit earlier than expected two weeks ago, but
that’s definitely not tomorrow and also not next week.

_Potential return date: Late December/early January_

## **Joe Gomez**

Joe Gomez started in Liverpool’s win over Brighton but lasted just 26 minutes
before coming off injured.

He went down off the ball and briefly received treatment before hobbling off
with the club’s trainers.

It is yet another blow for the player, who has been injury-prone throughout his
career and has already missed long stretches of this campaign.

Speaking on Friday, Slot said: “Joe is not in the squad as well for the
weekend.”

_Potential return date: Unknown_

## **Wataru Endo**

Wataru Endo’s injury was revealed in a double update alongside Gakpo.

Endo may only be a bit-part player for Liverpool this season, but as injuries
mount he could have provided a useful depth option for Slot.

Unfortunately, it was confirmed ahead of the Brighton match that the Japan
international had sustained an ankle injury, and would be out for a matter of
weeks.

Slot said: "It's going to be weeks [for Gakpo and Endo]. Cody has a muscle
injury, and Wata has an ankle injury."

_Potential return date: January 2026_

## **Giovanni Leoni**

Giovanni Leoni suffered an ACL injury on Liverpool debut in the Carabao Cup
third-round win over Southampton and is expected to be out for the remainder of
the season.

“He is not in a good place, of course, because he tore his ACL and it means he
will be out for a year,” Slot said of the young Italian. “Being so young and
coming to a new country and playing so well in your first game... it's very hard
to take the positive side.

“There's never a positive side - but you always try to look at a positive side
and that is that he is still so young, so he has so many years still to go after
he recovers from a terrible injury like that.”

_Potential return date: Autumn 2026_

# Liverpool accused of 'dereliction of care' over Alexander Isak injury in

## The Liverpool forward is set for a prolonged period on the sidelines after
suffering a broken leg in the win over Tottenham, but there has been a backlash
after he walked off the pitch, supported by club medical staff

![Alexander Isak faces a long lay-off after breaking his leg in Liverpool's win
over
Tottenham](https://i2-prod.liverpool.com/article33113660.ece/ALTERNATES/s1023/0_GettyImages-2252231734.jpg)

Jason Cundy has criticized Liverpool's medical staff after Alexander Isak left
the pitch against Tottenham without the use of a stretcher(Image: Getty Images)

Former Chelsea defender Jason Cundy has slammed Liverpool for a “dereliction of
care” over the club’s handling of Alexander Isak’s injury.

‌  

It was confirmed on Monday that [Isak ](https://www.liverpool.com/all-
about/alexander-isak)sustained a broken fibula and also suffered an ankle injury
in the 2-1 win over Tottenham Hotspur. The ex-Newcastle United striker
[underwent surgery to address the issue](https://www.liverpool.com/liverpool-fc-
news/features/liverpool-confirms-alexander-isak-surgery-33108143), and Arne Slot
confirmed on Tuesday that he is not expecting Isak to return for several months.

‌  

The 26-year-old was in clear discomfort when his foot got [caught under Micky
van de Ven’s outstretched leg](https://www.liverpool.com/liverpool-fc-
news/features/slot-micky-vandeven-alexander-isak-33109622), as he scored the
opening goal of the game. Isak immediately signalled to the bench that he was in
real discomfort and had to be helped off the field by Liverpool’s medical staff.

‌  

READ MORE: [Thomas Frank fires back at Arne Slot over comments on Micky van de
Ven and Alexander Isak injury](https://www.liverpool.com/liverpool-fc-
news/features/frank-slot-van-de-ven-33114061)READ MORE: [Andoni Iroala makes
Antoine Semenyo transfer admission as Liverpool faces new
competition](https://www.liverpool.com/liverpool-fc-news/features/andoni-iroala-
makes-antoine-semenyo-33113610)

But Cundy was astonished that a stretcher was not called for to ensure his safe
withdrawal from the pitch.

“I find it astonishing that they are commodities worth multi-millions of pounds,
in his case, over £100M,”[ the radio host said on talkSPORT’s The Sports
Bar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=asYMTVBiHvM).

“It’s a dereliction of care that they got him to walk off that field. There was
a stretcher.

‌  

“You and I were doing the phone-in and I said ‘He’s walking off’ and you said
‘the stretcher is behind’,” he added, speaking to cohost Jamie O’Hara.

![ Alexander Isak of Liverpool leaves the game
injured](https://i2-prod.liverpool.com/article33105263.ece/ALTERNATES/s1200e/0_GettyImages-2252248473.jpg)

Isak walked off the pitch despite suffering a broken leg(Image: Marc
Atkins/Getty Images)

“I just do not get it. I cannot believe they made him walk on a broken leg. Just
think about that, a professional athlete, Liverpool has played well in excess of
£100M, and making him walk off the field to play with a broken leg, dereliction
of care.

‌  

“And it's not just Liverpool, by the way they're all at it,” Cundy continued. “I
just saw Bruno [Fernandes] come off the field to play yesterday; he's done a
hamstring and he's then walking back down the touchline.

“I've done a hamstring, I've done a bad one, like grade three, so his is not
that bad, but give him a crutch to walk. Protect the injury.

![Alexander Isak is treated by medical staff after going down injured during the
Premier League match between Tottenham Hotspur and
Liverpool](https://i2-prod.liverpool.com/article33100956.ece/ALTERNATES/s1200e/1_GettyImages-2252947741.jpg)

Isak was in clear pain immediately after Van de Ven's tackle(Image: Getty
Images)

Article continues below

“I don't get it. Straight after the injury, hasn't been assessed, wrap him in
cotton wool.

“Isak, limping off, he was in tears. You saw him. He knew he'd done something
bad.

“I was angry watching it. I can only imagine how he might feel now. It's their
job to care for his well-being, for his fitness and his health. Get the little
buggies on to protect that player.”
