Read the latest information on injuries, suspensions and availability in the
Liverpool men's squad.

​​​​​​ _Last updated on December 13_

  * ## **Injuries**

**Wataru Endo**

The midfielder has been out of action with an ankle injury since appearing in
the 3-3 draw at Leeds United on December 6.

Arne Slot said his absence will be "weeks" when providing an update on December
12.

**Jeremie Frimpong**

Frimpong was forced off during the early stages of the Champions League win over
Eintracht Frankfurt in October.

Slot reported on December 13 that "normally Jeremie Frimpong will train with us
[next week]".

**Cody Gakpo**

Gakpo is currently sidelined with a muscle problem. As with Endo, speaking on
December 12 Slot said the issue will rule him out for "weeks".

**Joe Gomez**

The No.2 was forced off during the first half of the victory over Brighton &
Hove Albion with a muscle concern.

Slot on December 13: "If I have a player who goes off with a muscle injury like
Joe, that's normally not a positive thing. I would not expect him to be in the
squad next week [at Tottenham Hotspur]. But sometimes you get a positive
surprise."

**Giovanni Leoni**

The defender is amid a lengthy spell on the sidelines after suffering an
anterior cruciate ligament injury at the end of his debut against Southampton in
the Carabao Cup.

Slot on September 26: "He is not in a good place, of course, because he tore his
ACL and it means he will be out for a year. Being so young and coming to a new
country and playing so well in your first game... it's very hard to take the
positive side.

"There's never a positive side – but you always try to look at a positive side
and that is that he is still so young, so he has so many years still to go after
he recovers from a terrible injury like that."

**Dominik Szoboszlai**

Szoboszlai was withdrawn in the closing stages of the win against Brighton due
to an ankle injury.

Slot on December 13: "It didn't look great when I just saw him. But he has
unbelievable mentality so let's hope for the best."

  * ## **Availability**

**Mohamed Salah**

After featuring off the bench versus Brighton on December 13, the forward
departs to represent Egypt at the Africa Cup of Nations.

His country's group-stage games are against Zimbabwe (December 22), South Africa
(December 26) and Angola (December 29).

  * ## **Suspensions**

**Conor Bradley** served a one-game Premier League suspension for the clash with
Brighton on December 13, after picking up his fifth caution of the season in the
draw at Leeds.

He therefore becomes available once more for the trip to Tottenham on December
20.

Bradley is also one yellow card away from incurring a one-match suspension in
the Champions League.

## Alexander Isak

[Alexander Isak](https://www.liverpool.com/all-about/alexander-isak) will be out
for the foreseeable future after suffering a broken leg [following Micky van de
Ven's late challenge against Tottenham](https://www.liverpool.com/liverpool-fc-
news/features/slot-micky-vandeven-alexander-isak-33109622). The striker
underwent surgery on a fractured fibula and ankle problem that will keep him out
of action for at least a few months.

Slot has [raised hopes that Isak will return before the end of the
season](https://www.liverpool.com/liverpool-fc-news/features/breaking-arne-slot-
gives-alexander-33108844), although any comeback would likely be late in the
campaign. Given he has already struggled to settle in after missing pre-season,
it's unlikely we'll see the best of him once he is back in the side.

‌  

**Possible return date:** April/May 2026

## Conor Bradley

Liverpool will have to make a late decision on [Conor
Bradley](https://www.liverpool.com/all-about/conor-bradley) after the right-back
was forced off at half-time against Tottenham. He appeared to be struggling with
his hamstring, and given his injury record, it's never a promising sign to see
him hobbling.

Slot played down fears that it might be anything serious, although Bradley is a
major doubt for Saturday's game. He missed training on Tuesday and Christmas
Eve, with his head coach [waiting to see whether he can take part in the
session](https://www.liverpool.com/liverpool-fc-news/features/breaking-conor-
bradley-update-arne-33108962) on Boxing Day.

‌  

**Potential return date:** December 27 vs Wolves

## Cody Gakpo

[Cody Gakpo](https://www.liverpool.com/all-about/cody-gakpo) has been out
injured since picking up a muscle problem during the 3-3 draw at Leeds earlier
this month.

‌  

Slot described his fellow Dutchman's situation as being similar to Bradley's,
while also suggesting that he could make his return against Wolves this weekend.
A decision on his fitness will likely be made on Boxing Day if he can train with
his teammates, but that could be a potential boost for the Reds.

**Possible return date:** December 27 vs Wolves

## Joe Gomez

[Joe Gomez](https://www.liverpool.com/all-about/joe-gomez) was forced off midway
through the first half of the 2-0 win over Brighton, having assisted [Hugo
Ekitike](https://www.liverpool.com/all-about/hugo-ekitike) for the opener less
than a minute into the game.

‌  

The defender has had more than his fair share of injuries during his time at
Anfield, and the sight of him limping off at Anfield won't fill fans with hope.
Slot confirmed on Tuesday that he will remain out injured for Saturday's game,
with no clear indication of when he might return at the moment.

**Possible return date:** Unknown

​​​​​​ _Last updated on December 13_

  * ## **Injuries**

**Wataru Endo**

The midfielder has been out of action with an ankle injury since appearing in
the 3-3 draw at Leeds United on December 6.

Arne Slot said his absence will be "weeks" when providing an update on December
12.

**Jeremie Frimpong**

Frimpong was forced off during the early stages of the Champions League win over
Eintracht Frankfurt in October.

Slot reported on December 13 that "normally Jeremie Frimpong will train with us
[next week]".

**Cody Gakpo**

Gakpo is currently sidelined with a muscle problem. As with Endo, speaking on
December 12 Slot said the issue will rule him out for "weeks".

**Joe Gomez**

The No.2 was forced off during the first half of the victory over Brighton &
Hove Albion with a muscle concern.

Slot on December 13: "If I have a player who goes off with a muscle injury like
Joe, that's normally not a positive thing. I would not expect him to be in the
squad next week [at Tottenham Hotspur]. But sometimes you get a positive
surprise."

**Giovanni Leoni**

The defender is amid a lengthy spell on the sidelines after suffering an
anterior cruciate ligament injury at the end of his debut against Southampton in
the Carabao Cup.

Slot on September 26: "He is not in a good place, of course, because he tore his
ACL and it means he will be out for a year. Being so young and coming to a new
country and playing so well in your first game... it's very hard to take the
positive side.

"There's never a positive side – but you always try to look at a positive side
and that is that he is still so young, so he has so many years still to go after
he recovers from a terrible injury like that."

**Dominik Szoboszlai**

Szoboszlai was withdrawn in the closing stages of the win against Brighton due
to an ankle injury.

Slot on December 13: "It didn't look great when I just saw him. But he has
unbelievable mentality so let's hope for the best."

  * ## **Availability**

**Mohamed Salah**

After featuring off the bench versus Brighton on December 13, the forward
departs to represent Egypt at the Africa Cup of Nations.

His country's group-stage games are against Zimbabwe (December 22), South Africa
(December 26) and Angola (December 29).

  * ## **Suspensions**

**Conor Bradley** served a one-game Premier League suspension for the clash with
Brighton on December 13, after picking up his fifth caution of the season in the
draw at Leeds.

He therefore becomes available once more for the trip to Tottenham on December
20.

Bradley is also one yellow card away from incurring a one-match suspension in
the Champions League.

Meanwhile, **Dominik Szoboszlai** is one caution away from serving a one-match
suspension in the Premier League.
