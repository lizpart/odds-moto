## The Magpies will assess why loan deal with youngster went wrong as teenager

Chief Newcastle United Writer

Antonio Cordero (R) smiles at the Newcastle United Training Centre alongside
transfer chief Steve Nickson

Newcastle United have recalled Antonio Cordero from an ill-fated loan spell with
KVC Westerlo was scrapped by the Magpies.

Cordero arrived from Malaga on a Bosman deal last summer after Newcastle fought
off interest from Barcelona last summer. The idea was for the 19-year-old to
play in the Belgian Pro League on a regular basis, build up experience and
return to Tyneside next pre-season aiming to challenge Eddie Howe's first-team.

[But Newcastle cancelled the loan deal for
Cordero](https://www.chroniclelive.co.uk/sport/football/football-news/furious-
newcastle-united-teenager-set-32867375) after he failed to start a league or cup
game and played just 102 minutes since the summer. Cordero will be recalled by
Newcastle today, but is likely to head out on loan again, with Cadiz one of the
options on the table for the teenager.

Westerlo have squandered a £1.8m loan fee after failing to make the deal work
for Cordero despite priding themselves as "the friendliest club in Belgium". Ex-
Toon sporting director Paul Mitchell spoke glowingly about Cordero last summer
after the capture from Spain and said: "Antonio is an exciting talent. We're
delighted to bring him to amidst interest in Spain and across Europe."

However, Chronicle Live understands that Newcastle will resist the temptation to
take a look at him in January despite having a few injury concerns. Anthony
Elanga missed out completely on the 1-0 loss at Man United after taking a whack
towards the end of the Chelsea game but our information is that the leg injury
is not severe.

Elanga will be back in the squad sooner rather than later and the injury is not
as bad as first feared.: "He took a knock against Chelsea. We don't think it is
too serious, and we hope to have him back pretty quickly."

With the January window just around the corner, Newcastle have fielded a number
of enquiries for some of their fringe players for possible loans. Both Alex
Murphy and Leo Shahar are wanted on loan by EFL clubs, but with injuries cutting
through Howe's squad, the Magpies aren't in a position to give any such exit the
green light.

Murphy spent time on loan at Bolton Wanderers last season but is needed as cover
due to an injury to Tino Livramento at left-back, plus longer-term absentees
Sven Botman and Dan Burn.

Right-back Shahar has been drafted in as cover and has taken a place on the
bench in recent games too as United's issues show no sign of improving in the
short term.

When asked if soon to be out of contract Kieran Trippier or Botman could be back
for Burnley tomorrow, Howe said: “Our backline has been really stretched and the
guys that have played have done really well, especially Lewis Miley playing out
of position.

“We’d welcome some of those players back. Whether it is for Burnley, I don’t
know.”
