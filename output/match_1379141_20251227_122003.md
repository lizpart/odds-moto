the alight with Sporting. The 27-year-old has made just seven appearances but

Parker is weighing up whether he is best used as a threat against a tiring
defence - something Everton may have to consider in the latter stages on
Saturday. Parker said: “If there's one person in this squad at this present
moment in time, after what he came out of last year, that can be probably hugely
disappointed it is Marcus.

“But what I will say is I've not seen [any concern] with his general
professionalism around the place. He's managed to get some minutes and we've
seen his quality and it's certainly something that I need to think about.”

Parker spoke highly of Everton boss . He said: “I have the utmost respect for
David. I met him a few times along our journeys and he has, firstly, been an
incredible man everywhere I've been in his company, and a real honest man. With
what he's done and how his teams play it will be a tough game against a well-
organised team and someone who has got vast experience. They've picked up some
really good results over the course of this season as well.”
