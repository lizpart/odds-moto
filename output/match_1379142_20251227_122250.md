# Nine players could miss Chelsea vs Aston Villa as Cole Palmer update issued

## Chelsea are gearing up to face Aston Villa on Saturday afternoon, with a
number of players set to miss the action on both sides

Cole Palmer should be fit to play the full match for Chelsea against Aston Villa

Enzo Maresca and Unai Emery will be forced to navigate a number of injuries as
they head into Saturday's clash at Stamford Bridge.

play host to Aston Villa in the Premier League at 5.30pm, in a match which could
end up deciding which club finishes above the other this season. As things
stand, Chelsea sit fourth in the league table, seven points behind the Villans,
who occupy third.

While both managers will have hoped to have a full-strength squad to pick and
choose from, matters are far bleaker on each side. Blues star should be
available for the duration of the match, as Maresca confirmed earlier this week
that his rehabilitation from groin and foot injuries has gone well.

However, a number of his team-mates will miss the fixture as they continue to
recover. _Here are all of the players who could miss Saturday's clash between
Chelsea and Aston Villa..._

  1. Levi Colwill is expected to sit out the rest of the season as a result of an ACL injury sustained during Chelsea's pre-season preparations
(Image: Chelsea FC via Getty Images)

  2. Mykhailo Mudryk is provisionally suspended by the FA after an adverse finding in a routine urine test. He has not played for the club, nor his nation of Ukraine, since last November.
  3. Romeo Lavia is currently side-lined with a muscular injury, and is not expected to return to playing until the new year.

  1. Maresca explained earlier this month that Dario Essugo had suffered a setback with regard to his recovery from a thigh injury. He is expected to miss Saturday's clash with Villa.
(Image: Chelsea FC via Getty Images)

  2. Earlier this month, Unai Emery confirmed that Ross Barkley could miss up to 12 weeks of action as a result of a knee injury sustained against Wolves. The former Chelsea star is not expected to play until the new year.
  3. Evann Guessand is currently taking part in the Africa Cup Of Nations with Ivory Coast and will miss the game.

  1. Pau Torres is expected to return at the end of the month after missing matches against West Ham and Manchester United through injury. He may not feature against Chelsea.
  2. Tyrone Mings has been nursing a thigh injury as of recent and is unavailable for selection until January.
  3. Harvey Elliott missed Villa's clash with United as a result of illness. He will be checked ahead of the Chelsea match.

# Delap, Estevao, Lavia: Chelsea injury news and expected return dates ahead of
Aston Villa clash

24th Dec 2025, 09:33 GMT

The latest injury news as Chelsea face Aston Villa in the Premier League | Getty Images/ Canva 
The latest Premier League injury updates from Chelsea as two absentees push for
a comeback against high-flying Aston Villa

It’s third against fourth in the Premier League this weekend as Chelsea play
host to a red-hot Aston Villa side at Stamford Bridge. The Blues came back from
two goals down to secure a commendable 2-2 draw against Newcastle United at St.
James’ Park, holding on to fourth place at Christmas in the process, but now
face strong pressure from a resurgent Liverpool who are creeping up the table
after winning three of their last five matches.

Chelsea had ambitions of challenging for the title at the start of the campaign,
but have recently experienced a dip which has seen them win just one of their
last five league matches. They are currently trailing third placed Aston Villa
by seven points, and will need to be at their best to secure a positive result
against a team that have recorded their best run in more than a century, after
winning a whopping ten games on the bounce across all competitions.

The Blues welcomed back Moises Caicedo to the midfield during their recent draw
with Newcastle following the Ecuador international’s suspension, while they are
also beginning to get more minutes out of Cole Palmer after a frustrating injury
hit start for their talisman. Enzo Maresca has missed a number of his key
players in recent weeks but could introduce two stars back into the fray at
Stamford Bridge this weekend after layoffs.

Here we round up the latest injury news involving Chelsea ahead of what has the
potential to be one of the standout fixtures of this season’s festive schedule:

## Will Liam Delap play against Aston Villa?

[Big summer signing Liam
Delap](https://www.3addedminutes.com/sport/football/chelsea/joao-pedro-liam-
delap-start-chelsea-fantasy-premier-league-5265737) has missed the last four
matches across all competitions with a shoulder injury that he sustained against
Bournemouth at the start of the month. It was the latest setback in a
frustrating start to life at Stamford Bridge for the former Ipswich Town
striker, who also missed six matches with a hamstring problem earlier in the
campaign.

However, Delap’s latest problem is not thought to be as bad as initially feared,
meaning he could be in contention for a return to the team in some capacity
against Aston Villa.: “Liam [is] probably available for Aston Villa. We said
since his injury, we didn't know exactly for how long, but he is progressing
well.”

Delap is yet to score in eight league appearances for the Blues this term. His
only goal across competitions came in the Champions League during an emphatic
3-0 triumph over La Liga champions Barcelona.

##  Romeo Lavia injury latest as Estevao pushes for comeback

Chelsea midfielder Romeo Lavia remains on the sidelines with a muscle injury
which is expected to keep him out until the New Year. The Belgium international
has struggled with numerous issues since making the move from Southampton but is
edging closer to full fitness and could be named on the bench against Manchester
City on 4 January.

Estevao is closer in his recovery. The Brazilian teenager is in a similar
position to Delap and could be included in the squad to face Aston Villa at the
weekend as he pushes to make a return from a muscle injury.

The same, however, cannot be said for Dario Essugo. He’s still waiting for his
Premier League debut after arriving from Las Palmas and hasn’t been named in the
squad since matchday three due to injury. The extent of the Portuguese
youngster’s injury still remains largely unknown and a return date hasn’t been
determined by the football club.

Academy graduate Levi Colwill is expected to miss most the season with a serious
ACL injury which jeopardises his hopes of a World Cup squad place with England,
while Mykhailo Mudryk remains a long-term absentee as well as he continues to
serve a ban for allegedly breaching Anti-Doping Rule Violations.
