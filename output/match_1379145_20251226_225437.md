## Bruno Fernandes injury news, updates

Fernandes was withdrawn at halftime of the 2-1 Premier League defeat to Aston
Villa on Dec. 21, an immediately worrying sign for fans who know how reliable
the 31-year-old has been and how rarely he is brought off the pitch.

Following the match, United manager Ruben Amorim would only confirm that the
injury to Fernandes was of the "soft tissue" nature.

"I think he's going to lose some games. I don't know for sure, so let's see,"
Amorim said.

The injury is likely to his hamstring, as he was seen feeling the back of his
leg shortly before the end of the first half.

## When will Bruno Fernandes be back for Man United?

According to [a report from John Percy of The
Telegraph](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/football/2025/12/21/manchester-united-
captain-bruno-fernandes-injury-month/) in the immediate aftermath of the injury,
Fernandes "could be missing for at least a month."

There was some serious caveating there in that report, but it at least provided
a starting point until Ruben Amorim gave his next update. At that point, the
United boss indicated that Fernandes would be absent for around three weeks.

"In three weeks, we'll have [AFCON participants] Amad [Diallo], Nouss[air
Mazraoui], Bryan [Mbeumo], the return of Bruno and Kobbie Mainoo, so we'll see,"
[Amorim said](https://x.com/SkySportsNews/status/2004482973060526082) ahead of
their Boxing Day match against Newcastle.

Hamstring injuries come in various severities, with Grade 1 the slightest with
just some slight tearing, Grade 2 the next level with a moderate tear and Grade
3 the most severe with a complete rupture.

A Grade 1 strain would leave Fernandes out for just a few weeks, while a Grade 2
would see him sidelined for a longer 4-6 week timeline. A Grade 3 tear usually
requires surgery and would leave a player out of action for up to three months.

Given Percy's report and Amorim's update, it's likely that Fernandes has either
a Grade 1 or Grade 2 injury.

A potential four-week absence would leave Fernandes out for a number of matches
in which United would have to replace their captain.

## How will Man United replace Bruno Fernandes?

There is simply no replacing the production Fernandes offers United both with
and without the ball. Yet Amorim will have no choice but to try, with Fernandes
out of at least the next few games due to injury.

The most like-for-like replacement in midfield would be **Kobbie Mainoo** , who
profiles as a similar player, albeit with far more muted overall numbers.
Unfortunately, the 20-year-old missed the Aston Villa match with an injury of
his own, and he is also sidelined for a few weeks.

Advertisement

Advertisement

Advertisement

Instead, the best option for Amorim is **Mason Mount** who has been used as a
quasi-No. 10 of late at Man United but has significant midfield experience from
the past, especially at
[Chelsea](https://www.sportingnews.com/us/soccer/chelsea) where he was primarily
a No. 8. With Mount moved back, it would likely mean that Benjamin Sekso and
Joshua Zirkzee could see more time up front next to Bryan Mbeumo ([currently
away at the Africa Cup of
Nations](https://www.sportingnews.com/us/soccer/manchester-united/news/when-
bryan-mbeumo-back-afcon-cameroon-schedule-man-united-
return/7879618320846cb903206136)) and Matheus Cunha.

A pairing of **Casemiro** and **Manuel Ugarte** at the base of midfield could
work, but this would need help from a creative standpoint moving forward. With
Casemiro suspended against Aston Villa, centre-back **Lisandro Martinez** was
used as an emergency midfield option, but Amorim will do his best to avoid that
scenario moving forward.

According to a report by
[CentreDevils.co.uk](https://centredevils.co.uk/transfer-news/exclusive-
manchester-united-now-actively-looking-for-january-midfield-signings/), United
will seek to find options in the January transfer window to help cover the gap,
but there's also not much the club can do in such a way unless Fernandes is out
for a more significant period of time.

# Bruno Fernandes to return in January, Kobbie Mainoo ‘the future of Manchester
United’ – Ruben Amorim

![Bruno Fernandes in action in Manchester United's white away kit, shortly
before he was forced off against Aston
Villa.](https://static01.nyt.com/athletic/uploads/wp/2025/12/21143025/GettyImages-2253005652-scaled-e1766345475289-1024x683.jpg?width=1920&quality=70&auto=webp)

By [_Colin Millar_](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/author/colin-millar/) and
[_Carl Anka_](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/author/carl-anka/)

_The Athletic has live coverage of_[ _Manchester United vs.
Newcastle_](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/live-blogs/manchester-united-vs-
newcastle-live-updates-premier-league-score-result/9JIgppaXVo7C/) _._

Bruno Fernandes will return from injury in January, Ruben Amorim has said, while
the head coach believes Kobbie Mainoo is “the future of Manchester United”.

On Sunday, Fernandes was replaced at half-time by Lisandro Martinez during
United’s 2-1 defeat to Aston Villa, a match which Mainoo missed with a calf
injury.

Advertisement

Amorim said Fernandes, 31, had suffered a “soft tissue” injury and speaking
ahead of the Boxing Day visit of Newcastle United, the United coach said both
the Portugal midfielder and Mainoo would miss the clash but should return within
“three weeks”.

“In three weeks, we’ll have Amad, Nous (Mazraoui), Bryan (Mbeumo) and the return
of Bruno, Kobbie Mainoo,” Amorim said.

Amad, Mazraoui and Mbeumo are all currently playing the Africa Cup of Nations
(AFCON), which will conclude on January 18.

United remain without injured centre-backs Harry Maguire and Matthijs de Ligt
for the visit of Newcastle, although Casemiro returns from suspension.

After hosting Newcastle, Amorim’s side welcome Wolverhampton Wanderers on
December 30 before a visit to Leeds United on January 4 and trip to Burnley
three days later.

United host Brighton & Hove Albion in the FA Cup third round on January 11, with
the Manchester derby at Old Trafford six days later — a match which Fernandes
may return.

Amorim also spoke of the future of Mainoo, whom he said “deserves and needs more
minutes” in his side while adding that the 20-year-old’s current injury was “bad
luck” due to the absence of Fernandes.

![Mainoo's first-team minutes have been limited this season \(Gareth

“Kobbie Mainoo will have the opportunity he has all the time,” Amorim said. “He
played in different positions and we talk about the position of Casemiro, he can
do that position.

“He can play, if we play with three, he can play like we play in the last game,
in the position of Mason Mount in this game (a No. 10 who drops into midfield).

“So, he’s going to be the future of Manchester United, that is my feeling. So he
just needs to wait for his chance and everything can change in football in two
days.”

Mainoo’s only start this season for United was in the shock Carabao Cup exit to
Grimsby Town.

Due to the volume of absentees against Villa, youngsters Jack Fletcher and Shea
Lacey made their United debuts.

Fernandes, the United captain, [made headlines before the
game](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/6893377/2025/12/16/bruno-fernandes-
manchester-united-transfer-exit/) after saying he felt “hurt” that United were
opening to selling him last summer.

What has happened to Premier League Boxing Day games?

[Manchester United](https://www.independent.co.uk/topic/manchester-united) and
[Newcastle](https://www.independent.co.uk/topic/newcastle-united) will be hoping
for a Christmas boost as they battle on Boxing Day.

football](https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/football/premier-league-boxing-

the sole [Premier League](https://www.independent.co.uk/topic/premier-league)
fixture on Friday. It is nevertheless an intriguing encounter between two sides
experiencing not dissimilar seasons plagued by inconsistency.

and eyeing a spot in the top five with victory. They were beaten at Aston Villa
last Sunday, though, and will be tested by the absence of Bruno Fernandes after
the midfielder [suffered a soft-tissue issue in that

transfer-news-injury-update-b2888783.html "How to replace Bruno Fernandes? This
is why Man United are in a mess of their own making") Can Newcastle take

## Manchester United Predicted XI vs Newcastle United

Manchester United return to Old Trafford on Boxing Day with Newcastle United
providing stern opposition, as Ruben Amorim continues his second season in
charge with selection problems mounting. Injuries, suspensions served and a
potential tactical tweak all shape the Predicted Lineup, making this an
intriguing test of United’s depth and adaptability.

Advertisement

## Injury Update and Squad Availability

The biggest concern surrounds Bruno Fernandes, forced off before Christmas and
now facing a potentially lengthy spell on the sidelines. Amorim has already
confirmed the scale of the issue, offering little room for optimism.

“No, not for this game,” said Amorim when asked in his pre-match press
conference if Fernandes and Mainoo would be available.

“They are recovering. I don’t think that it is going to take a lot of time.
Kobbie is going to return faster than Bruno.”

Fernandes’ absence could stretch to several matches, although the United head
coach remained deliberately vague when pressed further.

“I don’t want to say. I have an idea of course, but let’s see.”

Kobbie Mainoo also misses out with a calf injury, while Harry Maguire and
Matthijs de Ligt remain unavailable at the back. The return of Casemiro from
suspension, however, offers some balance ahead of the Newcastle United clash.

## Tactical Considerations Under Amorim

Amorim’s default 3-4-3 has come under quiet scrutiny, particularly after
Lisandro Martinez stepped into midfield at Villa Park when Fernandes departed.
That positional flexibility gives United options, though Martinez has not
started since February and may yet be eased back in.

Advertisement

Advertisement

Advertisement

If Martinez is deemed short of fitness, Manuel Ugarte is expected to partner
Casemiro, maintaining a physical core against Newcastle’s energetic midfield.
Sticking with three central defenders looks the safer route given current
injuries, with Luke Shaw, Lenny Yoro and Ayden Heaven likely to continue.

Out wide, Patrick Dorgu and Diogo Dalot are set to operate as wing backs,
especially with Noussair Mazraoui away at the Africa Cup of Nations.

## Predicted Lineup for Newcastle Clash

Up front, Amorim appears settled. Mason Mount, Matheus Cunha and Benjamin Sesko
are expected to retain their places, offering movement, pressing and direct goal
threat.

Manchester United Predicted Lineup: Lammens; Yoro, Heaven, Shaw; Dalot,
Casemiro, Ugarte, Dorgu; Mount, Cunha; Sesko

Injured: Fernandes, Mainoo, Maguire, De Ligt

Unavailable: Amad, Mbeumo, Mazraoui

## Match Details and Viewing Information

The Boxing Day meeting with Newcastle United kicks off at 8pm GMT on Friday
December 26, 2025. The match will be shown live on Sky Sports, with Old Trafford
once again hosting a fixture that could carry momentum into the new year.

With key absences testing Amorim’s planning and Newcastle arriving with
confidence, this Predicted Lineup reflects both necessity and calculated risk.

Newcastle United head coach Eddie Howe provided a positive update on Tino
Livramento's injury status ahead of the Magpies' Premier League trip to
Manchester United on Boxing Day (kick-off 8pm GMT).

Livramento missed Saturday's 2-2 draw against Chelsea after sustaining a knee
injury during last week's 2-1 win against Fulham in the Carabao Cup but Howe is
hopeful the right-back's tough setback is not as serious as initially feared.

Ahead of the Magpies' clash at Old Trafford, targeting a sixth victory in their
last seven meetings against the Red Devils, Howe also provided encouraging
updates on the likes of defender Sven Botman and Nick Pope as he prepares to
take charge of his 200th game as United head coach since his managerial
appointment in November 2021.

Here are some of the boss' main talking points from Tuesday morning's pre-match
media briefing at the Newcastle United Media Centre...

## On Tino Livramento...

"It's a difficult one because there's quite a bit of information on Tino. We're
waiting to get full clarification but, hopefully, it's not serious at all and he
can resume training quite quickly.

"Before confirming that, we want to be sure it's right. We haven't got clarity
on that but hopefully it's good news."

## On Sven Botman...

"Sven will be in the category of the group of players that are doing well. We
hope to get him back pretty soon. When that day is, I'm not 100% sure.

"He hasn't trained with the group yet but potentially good news. We don't think
he's too far away."

## On Nick Pope's availability to be in the matchday squad to face the Red
Devils...

"There's a chance. He hasn't trained yet but we hope he might train today so
let's see how he does."

## On their longest break between games, excluding international breaks, since
August...

"It's a strange feeling for us because we're in that rhythm of playing and, very
quickly, turning that around to prepare for another game almost straight away.

"It's been nice to have a couple of days without that intense feeling that you
always have when you're playing, giving the players a chance to come down from
what is always a high from playing and the adrenaline that comes with a match.

"It's been a good time but also an important time that we don't lose our rhythm
and that we feel regenerated. Since the last international break, bar one
performance, I think it's been really positive.

"Hopefully, they (the squad) get some time with their families but we will be in
training on Christmas Day."

## On Manchester United, who currently sit three points above the Magpies in
seventh place...

"Statistically, they're a lot stronger than last year. They've seen big
improvements in their general performances. They've been competitive and,
attacking wise, they have some big threats.

"I think it'll be a really good game, a tough game, for us and a good
opportunity at Christmas to enjoy the magnitude of these games. The atmosphere
will be special but haven't played them previously, I think we expect a slightly
different Man United."

## On Jacob Ramsey...

"That was his best game for us, against Chelsea. Athletically, very good and
some of the numbers he produced in that game were elite in terms of the
benchmark for midfielders so I think that's a great sign.

"The challenge for JJ has been, since his injury at Leeds where he twisted his
ankle, he had momentum then and had to start all over again. I think he's just
returning to his very best levels.

"I think the next step for him is to start impacting in the final third with
goals and assists."
