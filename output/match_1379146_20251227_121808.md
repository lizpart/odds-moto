## The latest from Nottingham Forest's treatment table as Sean Dyche provides an
update before Man City

Nottingham Forest midfielder Ryan Yates has been nursing an injury

Midfielder Ryan Yates is making positive progress in his recovery from a
hamstring injury, according to head coach .

Club captain Yates was forced off barely 15 minutes after coming on as a half-
time substitute in the Reds' defeat at Everton earlier this month. A significant
spell on the sidelines was on the cards following his initial diagnosis, but he
could now be back sooner than originally anticipated.

The news is a welcome boost for Dyche, given that Forest have been hit hard by
absentees across recent weeks. And with Ibrahim Sangare now on international
duty at the Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON), having Yates available again in the
not-too-distant future would bolster options in the centre of the pitch.

"He's doing okay actually," Dyche said about the Nigel Doughty Academy graduate.
"I said it would be weeks rather than days, but he;s making really, really good
progress - and really positive progress.

"He's back on the grass, although not with us, but he's back on the grass
running and stuff like that. We're hoping it will be sooner rather than later.
At first, we were worried about how long it would be, but it's looking more
positive now."

There remains uncertainty about when striker Chris Wood will return from his
knee problem. The New Zealander, 34, has admitted [no timeframe has been set for
a potential comeback.](https://www.nottinghampost.com/sport/football/football-
news/nottingham-forest-chris-wood-injury-10709957)

Meanwhile, full-back is getting closer to being involved again. The defender,
29, has been missing since September after undergoing hamstring surgery, but a
return appears in sight.

Aina is unable to feature for the first-team until the new year, having been
left out of Forest's Europa and squads for the first half of the season.
However, he made a second appearance for the B Team on Monday night when he
started their Under-21 Premier League Cup win over Wolverhampton Wanderers.

"We want all the players back to fitness," Dyche said. "He's progressing well
and played in the B Team on Monday night and was fine - as in injury free.

"It was another step forward for him."

[Dyche's squad lost 1-0 to Fulham the same
evening](https://www.nottinghampost.com/sport/football/football-news/nottingham-
forest-fulham-sean-dyche-10719301) and will be aiming to bounce back when they
host Manchester City on Saturday (12.30pm kick-off). Goalkeeper Matz Sels is
likely to start at the weekend, after being on the bench at Craven Cottage,
while No.2 John Victor kept his spot between the posts.

"John has done well, to be fair," Dyche explained. "Matz had trained four days
in 11; he was suffering with a finger problem and a groin strain and had only
had a few days' training. And John had done well, so I said; 'Look, Matz, he
deserves another one'.

"We'll see. Matz is a very good goalkeeper, we know that, but John has done well
since I've been here. We try to give people a bit of a chance, but not
forgetting what Matz has done because he has been a top goalkeeper - and he
still is."

# Pep Guardiola shares major Rodri injury update as Manchester City pair set to
miss Forest

## The Spain international midfielder could make his return for Manchester City
on Saturday, but John Stones and Jeremy Doku are out

Updated 14:15, 26 Dec 2025

Man City head coach Pep Guardiola speaks with midfielder Rodri.(Image: James
Gill - Danehouse/Getty Images)

Manchester City midfielder Rodri could make his return to the team against
Nottingham Forest on Saturday.

The Spain international had his previous campaign ruined by an anterior cruciate
ligament injury. After making his return at the end of last season, the 29-year-
old has suffered with hamstring issues this term that have again limited his
involvement.

Rodri has only made four starts and has been sidelined since the 3-1 win over
AFC Bournemouth on November 2.

Speaking ahead of the trip to the City Ground, Guardiola shared a positive
update on the player's condition.

"Rodri is much, much better. Available or not, we'll decide today," he said in a
pre-match interview with in lieu of the usual press conference.

The news was less positive on Jeremy Doku and John Stones. "Doku and John still
aren't there, but soon they'll be back," said the City boss.

Guardiola's side visit Sunderland on New Year's Day in their next outing before
a potentially key clash with on Sunday, January 4.

City confirmed the players returned to training at the City Football Academy on
evening to prepare for the Forest fixture.

They have the chance to go back to the summit of the Premier League table with a
win there, as they start the day two points behind leaders Arsenal. The Gunners
will face Brighton & Hove Albion at 3 p.m. (GMT).
